## Introduction

The most profitable affiliate keywords are often not the biggest keywords. They are the searches that reveal a reader is already comparing options, narrowing choices, checking value, or preparing to buy. Small modifier words such as “best”, “review”, “vs”, “discount”, and “alternative” frequently signal that the searcher is close to a commercial decision rather than casually researching a topic. For affiliate websites, these modifiers matter because they directly affect conversion rates, page structure, merchant matching, and revenue per visitor. A page targeting “best espresso machine under £300” typically monetises differently from a page targeting “how espresso machines work”. The first query invites comparisons, affiliate links, pricing [tables]({{ 'making-money-from-creating-websites-containing-aff/making-money-from-cr-f649af-best-of-affiliate-pa-55feea-comparison-tables-cl-5eb30a/' | relative_url }}), and product recommendations. The second is mostly educational. Understanding which modifiers indicate buying intent allows a website system to prioritise higher-value topics, choose the right page template automatically, and connect readers to offers at the correct decision stage.

## Why modifiers matter more than raw search volume

Search volume alone does not reveal commercial value. A keyword modifier changes the psychology of the search.

Someone searching:

* “what is cloud storage”
* “how does cloud storage work”

is still understanding the category.

Someone searching:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--comparison" markdown="1">

* “best cloud storage for photographers”
* “Dropbox vs Google Drive”
* “pCloud review”
* “cheap cloud backup deal”

</div>

is much closer to taking action.

Commercial modifiers work because they compress buying-stage information into short phrases. Research on buyer-intent keywords repeatedly identifies words like “best”, “review”, “top”, “vs”, “pricing”, “discount”, and “buy” as indicators that the user has moved into evaluation or purchase mode. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.rediem.co/post/purchase-buyer-intent-keywords" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: rediem.co">[rediem.co]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">rediem.co</span><span class="citation-popover-title">purchase buyer intent keywords</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">These queries reflect...Read more...</span></span></span> <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.keywordinsights.ai/blog/buyer-intent-keywords/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: keywordinsights.ai">[3keywordinsights.ai]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">keywordinsights.ai</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Buyer Intent Keywords [How to Identify &amp; Optimise For Them</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">February 18, 2025 — 3 Jun 2025 — Such queries may include words like “best,” “review,” “buy,” “discount,” or” “vs” that indicate they&#x27;re...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 18, 2025</span></span></span> <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://seranking.com/blog/search-intent/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: seranking.com">[SE Ranking]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">seranking.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">SE RankingThe 6 Types of Search Intent (Including the New...Transactional keyword modifiers are buy, subscribe, price, cheap, discount...</span></span></span>

For affiliate publishers, these modifiers are operational signals. They influence:

* expected click-through rates on affiliate links
* likely conversion rates after the click
* suitable content formats
* merchant selection
* monetisation priority inside a content queue
* [internal linking]({{ 'making-money-from-creating-websites-containing-aff/making-money-from-cr-f649af-affiliate-internal-l-d8b176/' | relative_url }}) direction

## “Best” modifiers usually signal shortlist behaviour

The word “best” is one of the strongest commercial modifiers in affiliate SEO because it implies the reader already accepts the product category and now wants help selecting an option.

Examples include:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--metric" markdown="1">

* best standing desk for small flats
* best VPN for streaming UK content
* best running shoes for flat feet
* best accounting software for freelancers

</div>

These searches usually indicate three things simultaneously:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--step-flow" markdown="1">

1. The reader intends to buy eventually.
2. They are comparing options.
3. They want filtering help.

</div>

That combination is ideal for affiliate monetisation because comparison-driven content naturally supports affiliate links, ranked lists, [comparison tables]({{ 'making-money-from-creating-websites-containing-aff/making-money-from-cr-f649af-best-of-affiliate-pa-55feea-comparison-tables-cl-5eb30a/' | relative_url }}), and product summaries.

Industry analyses of commercial-intent keywords consistently categorise “best” and “top” as evaluation-stage modifiers associated with commercial investigation. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.keywordinsights.ai/blog/buyer-intent-keywords/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: keywordinsights.ai">[keywordinsights.ai]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">keywordinsights.ai</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Buyer Intent Keywords [How to Identify &amp; Optimise For Them</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">February 18, 2025 — 3 Jun 2025 — Such queries may include words like “best,” “review,” “buy,” “discount,” or” “vs” that indicate they&#x27;re...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 18, 2025</span></span></span> <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://seranking.com/blog/search-intent/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: seranking.com">[SE Ranking]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">seranking.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">SE RankingThe 6 Types of Search Intent (Including the New...Transactional keyword modifiers are buy, subscribe, price, cheap, discount...</span></span></span>

### How “best” changes page structure

“Best” queries work best with structured buying-guide pages rather than essay-style articles.

Typical high-converting layouts include:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--benefit" markdown="1">

* quick comparison table near the top
* ranked recommendations
* “best for” segmentation
* pricing ranges
* pros and cons
* suitability summaries
* clear affiliate buttons

</div>

The modifier itself determines the expected user experience. Someone searching “best web hosting for beginners” expects recommendations quickly. Long theoretical introductions usually reduce commercial efficiency because the searcher is already in evaluation mode.

### Why “best for” modifiers are especially valuable

Adding a use-case qualifier often increases conversion intent further:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* best laptop for architecture students
* best mattress for side sleepers
* best protein powder for women over 50

</div>

These longer modifiers reveal a specific buying context. That allows affiliate systems to match:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--phrase-pills" markdown="1">

* narrower merchant offers
* more precise recommendations
* higher-converting product selections
* targeted internal links

</div>

Long-tail “best for” pages also scale efficiently because the template logic is reusable across many adjacent subtopics.

## Review modifiers signal trust-validation intent

The word “review” usually appears later in the buying cycle than broad comparison terms.

A user searching:

* “NordVPN review”
* “Herman Miller Aeron review”
* “Surfer SEO review”

is often validating a near-final decision.

Research on buyer-intent keywords frequently places “review” in the commercial-to-transactional range because the reader is evaluating risk before purchase. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.keywordinsights.ai/blog/buyer-intent-keywords/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: keywordinsights.ai">[keywordinsights.ai]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">keywordinsights.ai</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Buyer Intent Keywords [How to Identify &amp; Optimise For Them</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">February 18, 2025 — 3 Jun 2025 — Such queries may include words like “best,” “review,” “buy,” “discount,” or” “vs” that indicate they&#x27;re...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 18, 2025</span></span></span> <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://seranking.com/blog/search-intent/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: seranking.com">[SE Ranking]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">seranking.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">SE RankingThe 6 Types of Search Intent (Including the New...Transactional keyword modifiers are buy, subscribe, price, cheap, discount...</span></span></span>

### Why reviews convert well for affiliates

Review searches are commercially powerful because the searcher often already knows:

* the category
* the brand
* the approximate price
* the alternatives

The remaining barrier is confidence.

That changes the content requirements. A review page must reduce uncertainty rather than merely introduce the product.

High-performing [affiliate review]({{ 'making-money-from-creating-websites-containing-aff/making-money-from-cr-f649af-affiliate-review-pag-f66da7/' | relative_url }}) pages usually focus on:

* real strengths and weaknesses
* who the product suits
* who should avoid it
* pricing clarity
* setup or usability concerns
* comparisons against close alternatives
* screenshots, testing, or experience [evidence]({{ 'making-money-from-creating-websites-containing-aff/making-money-from-cr-f649af-best-of-affiliate-pa-55feea-evidence-blocks-affi-b0d439/' | relative_url }})

Generic praise reduces credibility. Commercial readers searching for reviews are actively looking for friction points and hidden drawbacks.

### Product-aware queries often outperform broad category terms

A review modifier attached to a specific product frequently converts better than broader category traffic.

For example:

* “best project management software” may attract broad exploration traffic.
* “ClickUp review” often attracts someone already deciding between a few tools.

That makes review modifiers valuable for affiliate systems focused on revenue per visitor rather than maximum traffic.

## “Vs” modifiers capture final-stage comparison traffic

* Squarespace vs WordPress

### Why these modifiers behave differently

Price-focused modifiers create a different monetisation environment from review or “best” searches.

The reader often prioritises:

* current pricing
* voucher availability
* retailer trust
* stock availability
* renewal costs
* shipping or subscription terms

This changes page design substantially.

A “deal” page often needs:

* rapid updates
* visible timestamps
* merchant comparisons
* coupon integration
* urgency cues
* price tracking

Static evergreen content performs less effectively here because the intent is time-sensitive.

### Cheap does not always mean low-value traffic

Many affiliates incorrectly assume “cheap” keywords are low quality.

In practice, “cheap” often means:

* budget-conscious
* value-focused
* comparison-heavy

These users may still convert strongly if the page helps them avoid poor purchases.

Queries like:

* best cheap web hosting
* affordable office chair for back pain
* budget mirrorless camera review

can monetise well because the user is still in active selection mode.

* Shopify alternatives

* Grammarly alternative for students
* alternatives to Mailchimp

These searches often appear when:

* pricing becomes a concern
* features disappoint
* users outgrow a platform
* subscriptions increase
* switching costs are being evaluated

* comparison pages
* reviews
* pricing pages
* migration tutorials
* “best” roundups

This creates commercially useful internal-link clusters around buying-stage intent rather than around purely informational traffic.

### SERP structure is often the real intent signal

Google’s results frequently reveal the real classification.

If a query returns:

* affiliate roundups
* product grids
* comparison pages
* shopping widgets

the commercial intent is usually strong.

If the results are dominated by:

* Wikipedia
* tutorials
* definitions
* forum explanations

the keyword may be more informational despite containing apparent buying language.

This matters for scalable website systems because modifier detection alone can misclassify pages. Combining modifier patterns with SERP-type analysis usually produces better monetisation targeting.
